Adaptation: dark armor and palette, gray beard/eyebrows, cape, shoulder details,
eclipse emblem, large hammer, black feather wings and eleven adapted animations.
Body, head and bone-attached pieces use a Generic skeleton with baked animations.
Wing deployment and small flaps are animated by BossPresentation in Unity.
Editable source: Art/Blender/CustodioDelEclipse.blend (Blender 5.3).
Exports: Custodio.fbx and CustodioWings.fbx.
Ketheric Thorm was a visual reference; no Baldur's Gate 3 model or texture is included.
